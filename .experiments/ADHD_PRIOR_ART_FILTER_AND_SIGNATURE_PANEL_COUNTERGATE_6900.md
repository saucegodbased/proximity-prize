## Decision

Six of the seven labels are either old mechanisms under new names or process
wrappers around the already isolated source-owned eliminant gap. The only
meaningfully new formulation is the fixed original-node **signature panel**,
but the premise-level implication it needs is false: selected degree,
large agreement, fixed-row badness, and projective-high received directions
can coexist with a family on which **every single node already separates all
selected polynomials**. Separation then leaves no equal-signature pair on
which to book the proposed difference factor, while one extension-field
value has far too much alphabet capacity to count the family.

This is not a counterexample to the complete projective-high DataEleven
incidence leaf. A surviving panel theorem must visibly use an additional
same-leaf field, not merely the four benchmark-facing incidence/projective
facts. In particular, the exact current target is still a source-owned
coefficient-locus cover or nonidentity eliminant with paid identity fibres.

## New highest-information binary test

The new executable is

```text
.experiments/adhd_signature_panel_separator_gate_6900.py
```

It builds an exact structural chamber over

```text
K = GF(17^6),
n=12, w=5, A=7,
E=X^2-alpha,
U0(x)=x/E(x), U1(x)=1/E(x).
```

The modulus `X^6+2X^2+X+1` is rechecked by the same Rabin identities as the
archived full-six control. `E` is root-free on the twelve base-field nodes.
For every nonzero `(a,b)`, the canonical interpolant of

```text
(a*x+b)/E(x)
```

has degree at least ten: if its degree were at most nine, then
`E*V-(a*X+b)` would have degree below twelve and twelve distinct roots, hence
would be zero; the degree-two `E` would divide a nonzero affine polynomial.
Thus this is a literal projective-high received pair at the scaled threshold
`n-deg(E)=10`.

For each seven-node support `S`, interpolate `U0` and `U1` on `S`. In all
`C(12,7)=792` cases the degree-six coefficient of the `U1` interpolant is
nonzero. There is therefore a unique `gamma` cancelling the degree-six
coefficient of `U0+gamma*U1`, producing a selected polynomial `P` of degree
at most five. Its actual agreement set is exactly `S`: the nonzero polynomial

```text
E*P-(X+gamma)
```

has degree at most seven and already has the seven roots in `S`. The same
nonzero degree-six coefficient proves that the fixed received row `U1` is
outside the degree-five projected RS code on every owned support. After
deterministically keeping one support for each seed, the same-witness family
has 552 distinct seeds and selected polynomials.

The exhaustive panel result is stronger than requested:

```text
each of the 12 one-node evaluation maps P |-> P(x_i) is injective;
maximum one-node fibre = 1;
collision pairs after the first lex test = 0.
```

So the exact facts named by the panel proposal do not force even one reusable
equal-signature pair. This exposes the central direction error: **separating**
the objects is easy, but separation into extension-field values is not a
small-image theorem. Difference-factor savings exist only before separation,
inside fibres whose total mass and nonidentity status still have to be
proved and paid.

### Scope

The chamber does not instantiate the enormous DataEleven retained mass, the
fixed-scalar residual payload, or the misleadingly named hard-corner field.
It therefore does not refute a theorem genuinely using those fields. Target
mass alone cannot repair the tested implication because one node already has
`p^6` possible values, vastly more than the target allowance. A future GREEN
must identify the additional same-leaf equation that compresses this alphabet
or forces large equal-signature fibres.

## Genericity, witness, determinant, and charging audit

1. **Genericity:** a relation nonzero over a function field may become the
   zero polynomial on a selected ground fibre. Every identity specialization
   must be assigned to the explicit exceptional mass; counting identity
   *values* is not enough.
2. **Witness switching:** all useful statements must consume
   `ProjectiveHighDataElevenHighEClosedIncidenceLeaf` (or its exact fields) on
   the original `U/Gamma/agreement/selected`. Period-two norms, independently
   chosen supports, or a fresh quotient family cannot be imported by name.
3. **Determinant degree:** characteristic-polynomial/norm arguments must keep
   the leading/Fitting boundary. The safe resultant degree is
   `delta*81+j*H`; a reduced rational norm can cancel a finite zero against a
   pole at infinity.
4. **Duplicate charging:** a locator factor belongs to a pair or fibre, not
   automatically to each candidate. Lex-first assignment can make ownership
   disjoint, but it cannot turn singleton fibres into active relations.
   Signed cycles likewise cannot subtract overlap for which only upper bounds
   are known.
