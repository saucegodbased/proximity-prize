## Divergence protocol

Five isolated generators used the frames `remove the load-bearing
assumption`, `speedrunner`, `hardware`, `biology`, and `regulator`. They
produced 30 proposals without seeing one another. An independent critic then
scored novelty, viability, and endpoint fit and rejected any proposal that
was generic-only, local-only, witness-switching, degree-unbudgeted, or based
on unproved incidence amortization. A separate agent clustered the ideas by
mathematical mechanism, and three top mechanisms were deepened independently.

This prevents the prior process error in which many differently worded
variants of one local rank argument were mistaken for independent routes.

## Clusters and ruthless triage

### Determinantal elimination

Fitting ideals, quotient norms/characteristic polynomials, localized
determinant tricks, and sparse Macaulay minors formed one cluster. Most are
the same idea at different abstraction levels. A bare Fitting support or
maximal minor is RED as a claim of progress: it supplies neither the required
nonidentity nor a degree/identity-fibre budget. The surviving experiment is
the quotient-norm binary gate: first prove that the exact agreement quotient
is finite of rank at most `42,700,739`; stop immediately if it is not. Only
then is a characteristic polynomial worth constructing.

### Separation and certified charging

Signature panels, first-separating coefficients, lexicographic coefficient
strata, Ferrers shadows, and decision trees formed the largest cluster. The
best implementation is a proof-carrying decision DAG: each split is literally
`c=0` versus `c!=0`, each object follows one route, and every leaf returns a
locus voucher or an identity-fibre invoice. This is strong process
infrastructure but not itself a counting theorem. Its useful binary gate is
whether the existing no-adjacent-hard-corner condition actually implies a
disjoint Ferrers-shadow/first-separator inequality on the original
coefficients. Without that implication, the entire cluster is bookkeeping.

### Cycle cancellation

Casoratians, convolution registers, signed bipartite cycles, and switching
networks formed one cluster. The smallest honest test is the four-edge cycle
on two selected polynomials and two common agreement coordinates. Evaluation
values cancel automatically; the key question is whether the **original
coefficient polynomial** is forced nonzero. If a finite countermodel makes it
identically zero under all currently retained hypotheses, discard this
cluster rather than extrapolating from evaluation cancellation.

### Intersection/specialization

Chow forms, polar systems, coordinate-free exterior minors, and deformation
to a generic fibre formed one cluster. These scored high for novelty but low
for viability. Existing controls already show that specialization can gain
excess/identity components, and no current multidegree stays under the exact
budget. This cluster is archived until a proposed theorem includes a literal
special-fibre multiplicity and degree receipt.

### Syndrome/dataflow

Streaming the transposed incidence matrix, parity-check pipelines, and
rotating banks were metaphors for global syndrome aggregation. They do not
produce a permitted algebraic certificate or a disjoint charge and are RED
unless translated into one of the three concrete gates above.

## Stop rules

An experiment earns endpoint-proximity credit only if its output applies to
the lossless same-witness record and contains the exact nonidentity,
degree/incidence, and exceptional-fibre receipts. Local rank, source surplus,

branch immediately.
