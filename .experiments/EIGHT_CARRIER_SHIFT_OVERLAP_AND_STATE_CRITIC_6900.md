# Eight-carrier shift overlap and complete-state critic

Date: 2026-09-13 UTC. Scope: lower-6900 research only. No production,
submission, score, radius, or claim file was changed.

## Verdict

The order-four module and its one-layer local determinant remain genuine,
but the proposed repetition of that `24 x 24` block through 2621 passive
shifts is **not valid as stated**. The eight diagonal rows of one layer live
at different physical seed exponents. After multiplying by `Z`, they collide
with diagonal rows of other carriers in the preceding layer. Consequently
there are not 24 new selected coordinates per error and per seed layer.

An exact two-layer F101 calculation finds a stronger failure: the overlapping
selected rows do not determine even the complete contact image of the
eight-carrier inputs. There is an explicit nonzero combination which is zero
on every proposed selected row but nonzero on 22--23 other contact rows. Thus
the scalar Toeplitz theorem in `TerminalSeedToeplitzSolve6900.lean` cannot be
applied to eight independently varying coefficient families by citing the
one-layer determinant.

This does **not** refute the Full187 order-four module. The complete
lower-grade image could absorb the hidden leakage, or a smaller compound
three-RHS submodule could avoid it. It does refute the claim that only
"strictly later leakage" plus the existing scalar Toeplitz lemma remains.
Absorption/confluence in the exact quotient is still the mathematical
theorem, not a mechanical final step.

## 1. The collision is forced before any field calculation

For

```text
(a,c)=(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1),(2,0),(2,1),(3,0),(4,0),
h=a+c,
```

the one-layer proof selects

```text
T^j R^c Z^(b+h),  j=0,1,2.
```

The `c=0` carriers have `h=0,1,2,3,4`; the `c=1` carriers have
`h=1,2,3`. For `K` consecutive `Z` shifts, the union of physical selected
rows at one error node therefore has only

```text
3 * ((K+4) + (K+2)) = 6K+18
```

coordinates, while the advertised coefficient-jet input has `24K`
coordinates. Already at `K=2`, 48 input coordinates are being tested on only
30 distinct rows. For the advertised target `K=2621`, the counts per error
node are

```text
input coefficient jets          24K = 62904,
distinct selected physical rows 6K+18 = 15744,
forced selected-map kernel             >=47160.
```

Across `e=81731` errors this is a raw overlap of 3,854,433,960 dimensions.
Redundancy of the parameterization would be harmless if all of this kernel
were contact-zero or lower-grade-exact. The exact test below shows it is not
contact-zero, so lower-grade exactness would have to be proved explicitly.

## 3. Why the existing ordering and Toeplitz lemmas do not close this

`TerminalSeedToeplitzSolve6900.lean` proves surjectivity for a *scalar*
convolution matrix generated by one fixed sequence `a`, with one new input
and one new output coordinate per shift. It contains neither the eight
relative seed offsets nor a quotient by lower-grade contact rows.

`HrsU0PCInteriorOrdering6900.lean` proves that each individual non-diagonal
PC term is earlier in an intrinsic order. Its live source equation explicitly
assumes `hIH`, namely that a functional already kills the aggregate
`passiveEarlierRemainder`. It does not prove that different source origins
give a confluent elimination, that their tapered coefficient windows are
stable, or that an earlier contact row lies in the image of the lower-grade
source. This is the same associated-graded issue recorded by the old `pi19`
STOP: an ordering of terms does not itself define a quotient map.

There is a second semantic issue with the phrase "modulo grades <=82". The
low-grade columns are not individually agreement cycles with zero prescribed
normal. One cannot quotient only their error-contact coordinates and forget
the simultaneous agreement and `J_YRS` equations. The quotient must be taken
after forming the full combined map.

## 4. The exact invariant that decides completeness

Let

```text
M = (C_G, C_E, J_YRS),
B = image under M of the literal source of grades <=82,
C_K = image under M of K legal shifted order-four layers,
P = projection to the proposed selected physical rows.
```

Define the hidden-leakage index

```text
kappa_K = (rank [B,C_K] - rank B)
          - (rank [P B,P C_K] - rank(P B)).
```

This is the dimension of carrier classes which are invisible in the selected
quotient but survive in the complete quotient. The selected coordinates are
a complete state for `C_K mod B` exactly when `kappa_K=0`. The local test in
section 2 is the special case `B=0`; it gives `kappa_2=18`, `kappa_3=29`, and
`kappa_4=38`.

This is the fastest decisive finite test for the claimed bridge. In each
faithful matched and offset chamber:

1. build `B` from the complete combined contact-plus-normal map, not an
   error-only surrogate;
2. obtain `C_K` as the intersection of the raw eight-family module with the
   literal legal source box, so tiny-width coupled cancellations are retained;
3. compute `kappa_2` first, then the full available prefix;
4. simultaneously test direct membership of the three `F0/F1/F2` targets in
   `B+C_K`.

A positive `kappa_2` says which extra leakage rows must be added to the
state. A zero result is meaningful evidence for the quotient theorem. Either
result is more informative than another one-layer determinant or width
audit.

## 5. Smallest honest theorem and recommended pivot

Complete-state surjectivity is sufficient but stronger than the benchmark
needs. The smallest THREE-RHS statement is direct bordered membership:

```text
for i in {0,1,2}, target(F_i) belongs to B + C_2621.
```

Equivalently, every dual functional annihilating both the low-grade image and
all legal shifted order-four rows must annihilate the three targets. This
formulation automatically keeps agreement contact, error contact, and the
polynomial normal coupled, and it avoids choosing a noncanonical
associated-graded representative.

Scheduling recommendation:

* freeze formalization of a repeated `24 x 24` Toeplitz block;
* run the `kappa_2` full-quotient test above immediately;
* if hidden leakage survives, either compute the small polynomial-matrix
  Smith/Popov state including the first leaked `E/S/T^3` channels, or pivot
  to the already observed compound terminal rows and prove the three direct
  bordered memberships;
* only use the scalar Toeplitz lemma after reducing to one fixed compound
  carrier (or prove a genuine multichannel banded-convolution theorem with
  its actual physical row indices).

The compound-row pivot is currently preferable: the benchmark asks for three
distinguished RHS, not arbitrary 24-jet output, and the finite offset shell
already says the eight structural families combine into one compound mode in
that chamber.

## 6. Full order-four module: valid repair, but rank 22 is an m=4 artifact

The natural completion of the eight families is the full weighted-order-four
osculating module. Besides the original eight, it contains

```text
J1^2,  V J2,  Lambda J2,

J2 = Lambda^2 V2 - 2 Lambda Lambda' V1
     + (2(Lambda')^2-Lambda Lambda'')V.
```

These three generators are **valid**; the prior STOP notes do not exclude
them.

* The `C3(A)` STOP concerns a different attempted differentiated
  prolongation. It drops the necessary third-jet term
  `-(A^2/2)V P'`, so its truncated expression has contact order only three.
  `J2` itself is the honest second osculating covariant of contact order
  three. Multiplying it by `V` or `Lambda`, each of contact order one, gives
  an honest order-four cycle without differentiating anything.
* The `J2-BZ` actuator STOP says that subtracting the pure-seed term leaves
  an affine `Y/R/S` form with an unavoidable `Lambda^2 S` channel, not a
  scalar value unit. The full `VJ2` and `Lambda J2` generators retain that
  coupled `S/R/Y/Z` content; they do not make the false subtraction. The
  stopped actuator actually explains why the `J2` curvature channel may be
  needed.
* The mixed-`H` checkerboard STOP constrains one specially coupled
  `J1^2` ladder with an error-locator prefactor and a locked first-jet ratio.
  It does not set an arbitrary `J1^2` multiplier to zero.
* The active-cubic pure-tail STOP assumes a particular intrinsic cubic and
  error-locator divisibility/cancellation condition. It does not classify
  the full eleven-generator error-contact image, and it does not include the
  `Lambda J2` active-linear family.

Algebraically, after evaluating `Lambda` as a unit at an error, the eleven
families are exactly the monomials of weighted degree at most four in
`V,J1,J2`, with weights `1,2,3`, homogenized by `Lambda`. They are therefore
the right *candidate* order-four module. The existing Full187 width formula
also extends: `J1^2` and `VJ2` have effective locator degree `d=2`, while
`Lambda J2` has `d=3`; their raw `S` terms are no wider than the conservative
`Y` bound. This still requires an explicit termwise width receipt before
production, but no old STOP makes the generators illegal.

### Hard go/pivot criterion

The current higher-m chambers have genuine grade-`<=J` defects
`(1,1,1)`, joint defect three, so they are the correct cheap discriminator.
For `m=5` and `m=6`, intersect all eleven raw families and their shifts with
the literal source box, then test the full combined bordered map.

* **GO** only if all three RHS lie in `B+C_K` in both the matched and offset
  chambers and the quotient leakage index `kappa_K` is zero (or yields a
  stable explicitly enlarged state).
* **PIVOT immediately** if either RHS containment fails in either chamber,
  or if a surviving `kappa_K>0` grows with `K` without a fixed finite state.
* Even two finite GREENs justify only deriving the symbolic quotient module.
  They do not justify target assembly. The next certificate must be a
  polynomial-module/Popov or Smith computation for the combined quotient,
  including invariant factors and endpoint delay. Full rank over `K(Z)` is
  insufficient: denominators/torsion can make a finite 2621-prefix fail.

The universal THREE-RHS theorem would then still have to quantify over the
target field, every received direction/residual pattern, all `81731` error
nodes, multiplicity 60, the exact tapered coefficient spaces, and the final
seed endpoint. After it, the independent `Z1` correction, the CS4/common-row
adapter, and the typed component/allocation proof of
`SelectedBadGivenSetsBound` remain. A finite full11 closure addresses none of
those bridges automatically.
