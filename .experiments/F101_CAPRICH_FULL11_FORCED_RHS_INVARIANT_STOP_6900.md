weighted-order-four terminal carrier module spans the required locator
residual. In the exact F101 chamber

```text
(n,w,g,m,D,s,t,J,L)=(10,5,7,5,35,2,1,8,12),
Q=Xi_E^2, E={7,8,9}, error-direction offsets=(3,5,7),
```

weighted-order-four covariants, through every passive shift of grades

three target classes.

Three explicit dual functionals annihilate the full lower source and every

selected-row overlap artifact and not a vacuous target test.

## 2. Exact legality, including coupled cancellation

Write

```text
V  = Y-QZ,
J1 = Lambda V1-Lambda'V,
J2 = Lambda^2 V2-2 Lambda Lambda' V1
     +(2(Lambda')^2-Lambda Lambda'')V.
```

The full order-four list is

```text
J1^2, V J2, V^2 J1, V^4, Lambda J2,
Lambda V J1, Lambda V^3, Lambda^2 J1,
Lambda^2 V^2, Lambda^3 V, Lambda^4.
```

Each is multiplied by `V^(m-4)`, by the unique extra `Z` needed to reach
grade `J+1=9`, and by `X^d`, `0<=d<9`. This gives 99 raw columns per layer.

They are not all individually legal at all nine degrees. In the displayed
family order the individually legal degree sets are

```text
0..4, 0..4, 0..4, 0..4, 0..3,
0..3, 0..3, 0..2, 0..2, 0..1, {0}.
```

The test does not discard the other columns. It forms the matrix of all 46
illegal raw monomial coordinates. That matrix has rank 37, so its exact
kernel has dimension 62. All 62 kernel basis sums are nonzero, legal raw
sources and have source-coordinate rank 62. Thus coupled cancellation of
illegal terms is fully included.

Different passive shifts have different total source grades, so their raw
illegal-coordinate supports are disjoint. The one-layer legality kernel
therefore gives the complete legal compound module independently in every
shift.

## 5. Consequence for the 6900 route

This counterexample invalidates a uniform structural lemma of the form

```text
complete lower source + shifted weighted-order-four cycles
  spans every locator-normal residual.
```

It does not by itself prove the literal Full187 target impossible: a theorem
using special target arithmetic, higher weighted-order covariants, or a
different coupled source module could still work. It does show that neither
more Toeplitz bookkeeping nor adding `J1^2,J2` to the current order-four
module closes the missing logical arrow. The next experiment should change
the producer space or exploit a proved target-specific constraint, rather
than continue formalizing this universal trellis.
