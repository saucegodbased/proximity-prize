at `A=57`, is exactly repairable on the frozen Full187 target.

The repair is not supplied by the apparently generous first lower-`Z`
fringe.  The exact missing quotient is instead controlled by

```text

delta P_pred = Omega^12 B,  deg B <  76965,
Omega = X^262144 - 1,
```

and reduces, after a common invertible Hasse diagonal, to


The resulting `54108 x 76965` high-quotient Toeplitz map has an exact
nonsingular `54108 x 54108` Hankel minor.  Together with the first `208036`
coefficient positions controlled by `A`, the map has rank all `N=262144`.

This closes the unique A57 capacity-prefix quotient at every node.  It does
**not** close the strict-lex tails generated by the sections.

## Polynomial-ring reduction

At an agreement node `alpha`, Hasse product differentiation gives

```text
H_12(Omega^12 V)(alpha) = (N*alpha^-1)^12 V(alpha).
```

This diagonal is nonzero and common to the own and predecessor variations.
The predecessor additionally selects one frozen `U`, contributing
`37*u1(alpha)`.  After dividing by the common diagonal and the normalized
scalar-column dual, the exact equation is

```text
A + 37*u1*B mod Omega.
```

Here

```text
dim A = 208036
dim B =  76965
N     = 262144
nominal surplus = 22857.
```

`A` controls coefficient positions `0..208035`.  On the remaining 54108
positions, multiplication by `u1` gives the Toeplitz matrix


## What is now closed

The 86 capacity-legal A57 scalar columns span every fixed-z A57 direction
except the one normalized quotient above.  The polynomial-ring gate supplies
that quotient arbitrarily at all `N` nodes.  Thus the complete A57 diagonal
can be solved: use the legal Pascal right inverse on the complementary image
and the `(A,B)` pair on the quotient.  Affine higher-jet values induced by
earlier sections merely change the right-hand side; surjectivity is unchanged.

This is the exact local statement:

> **LOCAL_BLOCK_GREEN.** Conditional on the already certified A58 prefix
> block, the A57 fixed-z diagonal, including its unique raw-window defect, is
> solvable over the literal frozen target with legal coefficient windows.

It is not a theorem that the entire Full187 tail, all lower active degrees,
or the benchmark claim is closed.

## Process update

The decisive workflow correction was to apply the committed scalar left dual
before spending time on a large polynomial fringe.  Doing so immediately
rejected the `339108>N` q11 route and exposed the smaller q12 source as the
unique physical direction that actually survives the quotient.  Future
outgoing gates should follow the same order:

1. enumerate the entire same-key/raw-column set;
2. quotient by the already legal scalar image;
3. retain only physical sources with nonzero dual evaluation;
4. then spend a Hankel/Toeplitz rank computation on their polynomial windows;
5. explicitly compensate the preceding diagonal and audit arbitrary section
   feedback before declaring GREEN.
