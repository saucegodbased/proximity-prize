# Full187 low relay: mixed-H associated-graded matrix STOP at weight 4

This note is restricted to `lower6900` research.  It changes no submission or
production source.  The exact executable receipt is
`full187_low_relay_mixed_h_ag_matrix_6900.py`; its integer arithmetic is also
mirrored in `Full187LowRelayMixedHAGMatrixStop6900.lean`.

## Complete legal row family and grading

At a simple error normalize `U=V=1`, put `x = V-1`, and retain the complete
`H^3 J1` tail as `z = H^3 Z J1 / U`.  Give `x` weight 1 and `z` weight 3.
The literal Full187 chart is `x = E + TR - T^2 S / 2`, so this grading retains
the order-three `H^3 J1` contribution rather than dropping it.  The target is
the normalized F0 value `1+x`.

The complete source-legal, pure-seed-cancelling family generated by the four
staggered relays and the two mixed-H binomials is

```
R_n  = (1+x)^n - z (1+x)^(n-2)                         (n=57,58,59,60)
Phi2 = (1+x)^60 - 2z(1+x)^58 + z^2(1+x)^56
Phi3 = (1+x)^60 - 3z(1+x)^58 + 3z^2(1+x)^56 - z^3(1+x)^54.
```

These are respectively the normalized forms of
`L^(60-n)(U V^n - H^3 Z V^(n-2) J1)`,
`V^56(UV^2-H^3ZJ1)^2`, and `V^54(UV^2-H^3ZJ1)^3`.
All four `R_n` were already source-legal (`n=57,58,59,60`); the two binomial
rows are the literal Phi_2 and Phi_3 additions.  Nothing outside these six
rows is included in the matrix.

## Agreement side (`C_G`), separately

This matrix is an error-side computation, but the agreement-side contact is
automatic for every row: `V` has contact 1 at `C_G`, `J1` has contact 2, and
`H^3Z` is a unit there.  Thus both terms of `R_n` have contact

```
(60-n)+n = 60,             (60-n)+(n-2)+2 = 60.
```

Likewise Phi_2 has contact `56+2*2=60`, and Phi_3 has contact `54+3*2=60`.
An `X`/CRT scalar multiplier has contact order zero and preserves these
statements.  This is only the `C_G` ledger: it does **not** construct the
unproved global CRT/Hasse lift.

## Consequence and scope

This is an exact STOP at associated-graded contact weight 4 for constant
effective amplitudes of every listed source-legal mixed-H row.  In literal
coordinates `x^4` has the unique `T^4 R^4` leading piece, while an `H^3J1`
tail is affine in the longitudinal direction and cannot supply that component.
Consequently ordinary CRT/Hasse multiplier derivatives cannot remove this
certificate without introducing a source block outside this six-row family.

The first unproved issue in the earlier weight-three GO was global
CRT/Hasse interpolation of its effective amplitudes.  This STOP is stronger
locally: even granting such a lift, it cannot extend the six-row packet to
weight 4.  A continuation must add a genuinely new legal normal source
stratum/block, then repeat both the error-side matrix and the `C_G` ledger.
