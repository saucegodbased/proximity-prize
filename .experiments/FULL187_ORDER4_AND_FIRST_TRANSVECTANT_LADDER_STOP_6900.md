separated cleanly.

1. The truncated quadratic prolongation
   `C3(A)=A*D(T2(A))-3*A'*T2(A)` is **not** order four in the literal

2. The pure first-transvectant ladder
   `H^a L^(60-2k) J_L^k` has many legal rows, including the new quintic

## 1. Exact missing term in the proposed quadratic prolongation

With

```text
T2(A)=A W^2-A' V W+(A''/2)V^2-(A/2)V P
```

and the honest rules `V'=W`, `W'=P`, `P'=P3`, product expansion gives

```text
A D(T2(A))-3A'T2(A)
= -3AA'W^2+3(A')^2VW+(3/2)A^2WP
  +(AA'''-3A'A'')V^2/2 -(A^2/2)V P3.
```

The last term cannot be dropped. In the smallest locator fixture
`A=X,A'=1,A''=A'''=0,(V,W,P)=(Y,R,S)`, the truncated expression is a literal
source polynomial. Applying the repository's actual
`localSeedSubstitution Rat 0 0 0` simplifies it exactly to

```text
Polynomial.C (3 * errorE * slopeR).
```

Here `errorE` has contact weight three and `slopeR` weight zero. Its
coefficient survives `seedContactTruncation Rat 4`, so this is an exact
literal countergate, not a generic Taylor heuristic. On the rational arc
`E=t^3/6,R=1`, the cubic coefficient is `+1/2`; the missing term contributes
`-1/2`.

To realize the full derivative construction one would need more than a
notation for `P'`: either a genuine third-jet source coordinate together
with the order-three chart that promotes the residual `E` from weight three
to weight four, or an existing literal mixed carrier that cancels `3ER`.
The current `(X,Y,R,S)` source and order-two chart provide neither. Merely
adjoining the formal term while leaving the contact chart unchanged does
not prove order four on the full contact ring.

## 2. Audit of the four valid quadratic order-four generators

The exact order-four active-quadratic span is generated by

```text

-A V T1(A),

T1(A)^2.
```

After `Q=H^2` and one scalar error-locator factor, their useful mixed tails
factor respectively as

```text

A^2 H^3,
```

Thus only the first and third literally inherit the old `L^58 H^3` common
factor. The other two have logarithmic-Wronskian quotients and only an

isolated mixed tails still overrun their source strips by

```text

-AVT1 YZ:        15437
2AT2 SZ:         15436
T1^2 RZ/YZ:      15436.
```

This isolated-tail calculation does not by itself exclude cancellation
among the four-generator span; the separate complete quadratic descent is
the relevant stronger STOP.

```text

```

The pure seed term `z=k` is the unique worst layer. Therefore whole-carrier


## 4. Error-image STOP

At an error root of `H`, every row with `a>0` has zero contact-weight-zero
part. Every surviving `a=0` legal row has `k>0`, so arbitrary scalar
multipliers and linear combinations still produce a function `P(J)` with
`P(0)=0` in the single local form

```text
J=L*w-L'*d.
```

The standard leading syndromes are

```text
F0=L^59*d,
F1=L^58*J,
F2=L^57*(L^2*s-2LL'*w+(2(L')^2-LL'')*d).
```

For `L!=0`, set `(d,w)=(L,L')`. Then `J=0` but `F0=L^60!=0`, excluding
`F0` from every such `P(J)`. Set `(d,w,s)=(0,0,1)`. Again `J=0`, while
`F2=L^59!=0`, excluding `F2`. `F1` is exactly `L^58 J` and is the only one
of the three in the one-coordinate image.

This is a weight-zero projection of the actual error contact, so failure
there already prevents equality of the full error syndromes. Adding more
pure powers, larger legal `a`, or scalar X multipliers cannot repair it.
